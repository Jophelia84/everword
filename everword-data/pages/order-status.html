Every order gets a confirmation email straight away, and a second email with tracking when it ships. Pieces are made to order and dispatch in 3–5 business days.

Checking on it

Use the tracking link in your shipping email. If it hasn't arrived yet, your piece is still in production — everything is made to order, so there's a gap between confirmation and dispatch.

Something's wrong

If your tracking hasn't updated in several days, or the estimated date has passed, contact us with your order number and we'll chase it.
