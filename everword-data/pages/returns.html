Everything we sell is made after you order it, and every order carries a card printed with your words. That means none of it can be put back on a shelf and sold to somebody else — so we can't accept a return simply because it wasn't wanted. We'd rather say that plainly here than at the point you're asking.

If it's faulty, damaged, or not what you submitted

We’ll remake it and send the replacement free of charge. That covers damage in transit, a fault we should have caught, the wrong item, artwork that isn’t what you submitted, and a parcel that has sat in transit for an unreasonable length of time. You won’t need to post anything back to us.

Film it, don’t photograph it. A short video showing the piece and the packaging is what opens a remake — still photographs on their own aren’t enough. Send it within 30 days of delivery, or 55 days if you’re outside the US.

Typos

Email us the moment you spot one. Pieces go into production quickly, so we can't promise to catch it — but if yours hasn't started we'll correct it free of charge, and if it has, we'll remake it at our cost rather than yours. Once per order.

What we won't produce

We won't print or cut personalisation that's unlawful, infringes someone else's trade mark, or is abusive. If we decline on those grounds we'll refund you in full.
