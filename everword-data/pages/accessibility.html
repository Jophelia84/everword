We want this shop to work for everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or browsers set to large text.

What we're working towards

We're aiming to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Practically, that means readable colour contrast, every control reachable by keyboard, meaningful alternative text on product photography, and forms whose labels are announced properly.
