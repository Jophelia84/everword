At Everword, we do not pull finished personalized pieces from a shelf. Every custom necklace, engraved bar, and bespoke keepsake begins as a blank canvas, waiting for your specific chapter to be written. The journey of your gift starts the exact moment you place your order.

Shaped to Your Story

Your words dictate the physical form of the jewelry. When you design a custom name necklace, the typography is precision-scaled to your exact spelling and capitalization. A three-letter name and an eleven-letter name are physically different architectural structures, each one cut from a single sheet and hung to sit at the collarbone. What you type is exactly what is cut — a true, unedited reflection of your sentiment.

Precision Craftsmanship

State-of-the-art precision cutting and engraving technology brings your design to life. Working exclusively with durable, everyday materials — stainless steel, radiant 18K gold finishes, and clear cubic zirconia — every piece is crafted with modern endurance in mind. For our wooden timepieces, the natural grain dictates the design, ensuring no two watches will ever be identical.

The Uncompromising Standard

We believe that a gift meant to mark a milestone must be flawless. Before any piece leaves the facility, it is inspected. A piece that does not pass is not shipped — it goes back and is made again, which adds about two days. This uncompromising standard takes a little extra time, but it ensures your gift is worthy of the occasion.

The Art of the Reveal

The magic of an Everword gift is in the presentation. Your custom message card is printed alongside your jewelry and placed face-up in the lid of our Signature Everword Box, or your chosen Luxe upgrade. It is intentionally designed to be the very first thing they see when they lift the lid — ensuring your words are felt before the jewelry is even worn.

The Journey Home

Because true intentionality cannot be rushed, your piece takes three to five business days to cut, plate, inspect and package, and production runs on weekdays. Then it ships, and delivery within the United States is a further two to five business days.
