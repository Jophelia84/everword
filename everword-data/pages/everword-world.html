A personalised piece has a strange property: it is worth a great deal to exactly one person and almost nothing to anyone else. That is the opposite of how jewellery usually works, and it is the whole reason this brand exists.

So the things we make are not aimed at an aesthetic. They are aimed at a moment — the graduation, the first year, the one that is missing from the table. The metal is the durable part. The words are the gift.

What you will find here

Names cut in script. Dates engraved down the side of a bar. A stone for a birth month. Three watches with something on the caseback. And a printed card in the lid of every box, because the object on its own has never been the point.

What you will not

Diamonds we are calling diamonds when they are cubic zirconia. Solid gold we are calling solid gold when it is a finish over steel. Sale prices that were never the real price.
