Metal

Our name necklaces are polished stainless steel, or stainless steel finished in 18K yellow gold. Our stone-set pendants — the Love Knot, the Pavé Halo, the Interlocking Hearts and the Halo Solitaire — are finished in 14K white gold or 18K yellow gold. Those are finishes over steel. Nothing here is solid gold, and we don’t describe it as solid gold anywhere. Steel is the reason these pieces survive daily wear, showers and sleeves far better than plated brass.

The finish available is listed on each product page, because it genuinely differs by piece.

Stones

Set stones are cubic zirconia, not diamonds. Birthstones are coloured crystal. Cubic zirconia is cut and polished like a gemstone and reads brilliantly in daylight; it is simply not a diamond, and we won’t imply otherwise.

Chains

Most pieces come on an adjustable 16–18″ cable chain with a 9 mm lobster clasp. Some keepsakes use a fine box chain; the four-sided bar uses a 16–22″ snake chain so it turns as it hangs; the Graduation Cap sits on 18–20″, and the Name Charm necklace on 16–24″. Chain type and length are listed on every product page.

Watches

The Open-Work is an automatic movement — no battery, wound by wearing it — with a skeleton dial, hardened mineral glass and a genuine black leather band, water resistant to 3 ATM. The Chronograph is 316L stainless steel with a copper dial and a quartz movement, also 3 ATM. The Wooden Watch is a 45 mm sandalwood case on leather with a Japanese quartz movement and is IPX-4 splash resistant only — keep it away from swimming and showers. All three have an engravable caseback and arrive in a mahogany-style luxury box with an LED.

Sensitive skin

Stainless steel is what most people with reactive skin get on best with, but we have not had nickel content confirmed to us in writing, so we won’t call these pieces hypoallergenic.
