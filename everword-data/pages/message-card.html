Every order includes a printed card, at no extra cost. It sits in the lid of the box, above the piece, so it's read before the jewellery is lifted out.

Cards are printed square and sized to sit in the lid of the box. Your message is set in the middle, where the chain and pendant don't cover it.

Writing it

You can write your own, or start from one of ours and change it. Choose the occasion on the product page and we'll suggest a few lines that fit — a birthday reads differently from a memorial, and the suggestions change accordingly.

Around 200 characters sits comfortably. Shorter is usually better; the best cards we've seen say one specific thing rather than three general ones.

Before we print

The preview on the product page is the proof. What you see there is what we print — there's no second approval step, and we won't email you anything to sign off. Read it once more before you add to bag; spelling is the one thing we can't undo.
